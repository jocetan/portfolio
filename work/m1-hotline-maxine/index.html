Case Study

M1 Hotline – Maxine

Maxine improved M1’s overall NPS scoring by capturing callers’ intentions, which improves agent productivity by freeing them to focus on more complex cases.

Project Type
Conversational UX
Role
UX Designer
Year
2023 - 2024
Media
Collaboration with AWS

Background

Built on Amazon Connect, Maxine is able to engage in more lifelike conversations with customers. Combined with M1’s Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) trunk services for high quality digital voice communication, Maxine will help improve end-user customer experiences by engaging them in open-ended conversations instead of menu-driven interfaces.

The deployment of Maxine is part of M1’s continuous transformation journey to be a digital platform. Working together with the Amazon Connect team, we have currently built the following scripts:

1627 General Hotline

Balance & Recontract, Payment Status Check, Tech Assist, Lost Phone, My M1+ App, 5G Intent & Number Port, Card on File, Transfer of Ownership

1693 Prepaid Hotline

Top-up, Promotion, Proof of Ownership, Maxx Recurring Payment, Port-in, Termination, Registration, SIM Card Enquiry

1622 Business Hotline

Lost Phone, Escalation, Billing, Change of Authorised Officer, Roaming Matters, Reconnection, Reconnection of Services, New Sign Up, Fibre Connection, Promotions
